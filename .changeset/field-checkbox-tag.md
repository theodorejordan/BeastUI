Field, checkbox and tag. Field: a label, a box with an optional unit, a hint and an error, wired with `aria-describedby` and `aria-invalid`; numbers have tabular figures and no spin buttons. CSS: `.b-field`, `.b-input`. Preact: `Field`, `Input`. Checkbox: a native checkbox drawn from tokens, with the mixed state. CSS: `.b-check`, `.b-checkbox`. Preact: `Checkbox`. Tag: filled, read only, neutral or warn. CSS: `.b-tag`. Tokens: `color.warning` and `color.on-warning`. Segmented control: options take the Button hover fill.
